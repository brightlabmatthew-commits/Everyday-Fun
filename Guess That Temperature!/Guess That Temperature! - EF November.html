HOW THIS WORKS

Instructions

Guess the temperature, then check your math skills!

  • 🌡️ Step 1: Look at the picture and read the question.
  • 👆 Step 2: Tap the temperature you think is correct.
  • ✅ Step 3: See if you were right, answer the “Talk About It” question, then tap the ➡ arrow for the next one. Use ⬅ to go back.
  • ➕ Step 4: After 10 questions, try the Math Challenges using what you learned!
BEFORE WE START

Materials

Just one thing needed for this game.

  • 🖥️ A device or screen where students can see and tap the questions.
