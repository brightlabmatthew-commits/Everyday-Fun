Writing Speed Warriors!

Race to write 15 words — then talk about them!

Instructions

Ready your pencils — it's a writing race! 🏁

1. A word will appear. Everyone races to write it down first.

2. The teacher checks who finished first, then taps "Time's Up!"

3. Talk together about the question that appears.

After all 15 words, there's a Bonus Challenge: fill in missing letters!
