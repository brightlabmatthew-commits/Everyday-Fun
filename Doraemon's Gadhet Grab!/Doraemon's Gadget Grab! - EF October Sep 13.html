Doraemon's Gadget Grab!

Unlock 7 mystery future-gadgets

Instructions

Today we're opening a gadget vault from the future! 🔐

1. Look at the closed vault door and guess what might be inside.

2. Tap the door to reveal the gadget.

3. Talk together about the questions that pop up.

There are 7 gadgets waiting for you. Let's grab them!

Gadget 1 of 7
CLUE

What is this gadget called? Take a guess!

The Anywhere Door

Open this door and step out anywhere in the world you can imagine — no walking, no waiting!

Let's talk about it!

  • If you had the Anywhere Door, where would you go first?
  • Would you rather open it to a beach or to a snowy mountain?
  • What color would you paint your own Anywhere Door?
