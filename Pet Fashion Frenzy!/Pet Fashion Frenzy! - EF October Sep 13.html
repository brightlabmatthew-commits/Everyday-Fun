Pet Fashion Frenzy!

Pick a pet and design its perfect outfit! 🐾

Instructions

Time for a pet fashion show! 🐾🎒

1. Choose your favorite pet.

2. Pick clothes, a collar, an accessory, and shoes for your pet, one at a time.

3. Try any color you like with the color picker!

4. Watch your pet's outfit come together as you choose!

5. At the end, talk about your pet and its new outfit.
