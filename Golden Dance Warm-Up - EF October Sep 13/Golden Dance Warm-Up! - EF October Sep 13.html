Golden Dance Warm-Up!

Learn 6 real moves from the "Golden" dance-along, then perform the whole routine together.

HOW THIS WORKS

Instructions

Each move gets its own slide — work through them in order.

  • 🕺 Step 1: On each move slide, watch the big video clip of that move together as a class.
  • ✅ Step 2: Practice the move a few times, then tap "Mark as Learned" to unlock the next slide.
  • 🎬 Step 3: Once all 6 moves are learned, head to the Full Run-Through slide and dance the whole thing to the music.
  • 🏆 Step 4: Finish with the Challenges to make it extra fun.
BEFORE WE START

Materials

Nothing fancy needed — just space to move!

  • 🧍 A clear, open space where students can spread out their arms without touching anyone.
  • 👟 Comfortable shoes or socks with grip — no slippery floors.
  • 🔊 A device with sound turned up so everyone can hear the music.
  • 💧 A water bottle nearby — it's a warm-up, so we're moving!

MOVE 4 OF 6

Cross Open

Not learned yet

Complete this move to unlock the next slide →
MOVE 5 OF 6

Touch The Sky

Not learned yet

MOVE 6 OF 6

Grape Vine

Not learned yet
Classic side-step: step sideways, cross the back foot behind, step again, then tap — travel a few steps left, then right.

PUT IT ALL TOGETHER

Full Run-Through!

Play the whole video and dance every move back-to-back.

LEVEL UP

Bonus Challenges

Try these once the routine feels easy.

🧊 Freeze Dance: Teacher randomly pauses the video — everyone freezes in whatever pose they're in. Last one moving gets a silly forfeit (like doing the Grape Vine backwards)!
⚡ Speed Battle: In two teams, race to do all 6 moves in order, correctly, as fast as possible — no music needed.
🪞 Mirror Buddy: Pair up. One student dances a move, their partner mirrors it in real time like a reflection, then switch.
